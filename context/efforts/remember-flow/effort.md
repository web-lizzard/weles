## Goal

Close the `capture → distill → remember` loop at the link that actually turns written down into retained. Today distill produces flashcards that never return to the user on their own, which reproduces the "second junk drawer" failure one floor below notes. This effort delivers the review pillar: a single command opens a session over the cards that are due, the user grades their recall on a four-step scale, and the schedule moves on its own — so that everything due can be worked through in one sitting, and a card's next appearance is derived from the user's own grades, returning less and less often as it becomes known.
